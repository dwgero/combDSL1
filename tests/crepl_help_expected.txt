Commands (items in brackets [] are optional):
about                                 | display copyright and redistribution information
abstract [steps | ministeps]          | compute combinators such that applying <xyz...> to them
    ?<xyz...> = <expression>          |     reduces to <expression>
basis step [on | off]                 | names are converted to their stored expressions as a step
birds                                 | display the pre-defined bird combinators
colorize [on | off]                   | add colors to arguments while stepping
compare ?<xyz...> <left> = <right>    | compare applied normal forms within time limits
define [captured | live] <name>       | compute and store combinators such that
    [<xyz...>] = <expression>         |     <name> [<xyz...>] reduces to <expression>
dependson  [all] <name>               | display direct and optional indirect definitions that
depends-on [all] <name>               |     depend on <name>
depends on [all] <name>               |
exit                                  | end the program
find [all] [<num> | among <birds...>] | search for shortest combinator strings such that applying
    ?<xyz...> = <expression>          |     <xyz...> to them reduces to <expression>
help [brief | full]                   | display help information
inspect <expression>                  | display info about <expression> without evaluating it
key step [on | off]                   | after each step, wait for a keypress to continue
load <filename>                       | load a set-list journal from a file
quit                                  | end the program
references <captured | live>          | by default, capture name references at define time
                                      |     or follow later changes to them
remove <name>                         | remove a user-defined combinator name
revisions <name>                      | display every retained revision of <name>
save <filename>                       | save the set-list journal to a file
set [captured | live] <name>          | store <expression> as <name> with arity <arity> or 0
    = [arity] <expression>            |
show <name[@<num>] | all>             | display one revision of <name> or the entire set list
single step [on | off]                | display each step of the reduction without pause
step limit <off | num>                | limit ordinary and Single Step evaluations
usedby  [all] <name>                  | display direct and optional indirect definitions that
used-by [all] <name>                  |     <name> uses
used by [all] <name>                  |
usedby  path [between] <name1>        | display the dependency path between <name1> and <name2>
    [and] <name2>                     |
used-by path [between] <name1>        |
    [and] <name2>                     |
used by path [between] <name1>        |
    [and] <name2>                     |
